# Stages and gates

| Stage | Skill | Methods | Gate in one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Diagnosis | `problem-diagnosis` | S0 (13) | A decision statement, a facts-assumptions-unknowns register and hypotheses written before fieldwork |
| 1 Method selection | `research-orchestrator` | S1 (23) | Each research question mapped to one method, desk research first, at least one behaviour method |
| 2 Research design | `research-design` | S2 (13) | Signed brief, sample with size reason and deviant cases, second source per key finding, coverage limits |
| 3 Questions and fieldwork | `question-design` | S3 (11) | Past-event guides, pretested survey, checked translations, indirect sensitive questions, same-day snapshots |
| 4 Analysis | `insight-analysis` | S4 (23) | Two readers, an anomaly log, every finding traced to evidence, no percentages under n=30 |
| 5 Synthesis | `insight-synthesis` | S5 (14) | Four-rung insights that pass the three tests, a contradicted hypothesis or an explanation, a target choice |
| 6 Strategy | `marketing-strategy` | S6 (18) | Choices stated, what to stop doing, a positioning a rival could not copy, KPIs at three levels |
| 7 Communication | `communication-strategy` | S9 (7) | Objectives with audience and change, brand and activation layers split, researched messages, reach-first channels, budget logic, creative brief, test plan |
| 8 Measurement | `research-orchestrator` | S7 (4) | Baseline, a test or comparison, three-level reporting, retrospective filed |
| Local context (all stages) | method skills only | S8 (8) | Uzbekistan and emerging-market sources and checks |

The full gate wording lives in `plugins/marketing-research-system/skills/research-orchestrator/SKILL.md`. Method lists per stage are in [METHOD-INDEX.md](METHOD-INDEX.md).
